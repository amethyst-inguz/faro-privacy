# Faro — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 29-09-2026.*

Faro is a game made by Amethyst Inguz ("we"). This policy explains what
information is involved when you play.

## What stays on your device

Faro has no accounts. Your progress, settings and anything you type into the
game's writing practices are stored only on your device and are deleted when
you uninstall the app or choose "Begin the story again". Nothing you write is
ever sent anywhere unless you choose to share it.

## Game settings from the internet

Faro fetches a small settings file from Google's Firebase Remote Config
so we can make gentler changes without an update.
To deliver it, Firebase uses a random installation ID that
is not linked to you. Faro has no analytics and sends nothing about how you
play. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Advertising

Faro is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure
ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- device identifiers such as the advertising ID,
- approximate location derived from your IP address,
- information about ad interactions and app diagnostics.

This is processed by Google under its own policy:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's
consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at
any time in Faro → Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or
delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

## Sharing

When you choose to send a lighthouse card, Faro makes an image on your device
and hands it to your phone's share sheet. It is sent only where you send it.

## Notifications

If you turn on the evening reminder, Faro schedules a local notification on
your device. Nothing is sent to us.
